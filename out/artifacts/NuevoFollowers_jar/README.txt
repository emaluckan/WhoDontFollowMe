1. Go to Instagram from the browser > More > Your Activity > Download Your Information > Download or Transfer Information > Part of the Information > Followers and Following > Download to Device

2. Once they receive the email, they log in, and a . zip is downloaded, they unzip it and rename that folder as "instagram-user"

3. They place it anywhere they want.

4. Now go to the application, unzip newFollowers.jar and open unfollowers.bat, if it doesn't let you open NewFollowers.jar

5. Click on start, and it will ask you for a path where you unzipped the instagram file (eg: if you put it in C, the path would be C:\, if the path is Documents: C:\Users\YourUser\Documents\, etc)

6. Click on "OK" and all the users who stopped following you or never followed you will appear, right up to the same dd/mm/yyyy and hh/mm/ss

7. I hope it helps
